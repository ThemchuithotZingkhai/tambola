## Brand & Style

This design system drives an electrifying yet dependable social gaming experience centered on Online Tambola (Housie). The brand personality balances pulse-racing gaming excitement—the rush of waiting for that final ball, shouting "Full House!", and watching jackpot tickers spin—with institutional financial reliability. Because real money, UPI payments, and prize validation are at stake, users demand absolute confidence in fairness, transparent number generation, and seamless claim resolution.

The target audience spans diverse demographics: casual social players on mobile devices, community groups joining scheduled weekend games, and administrators managing high-volume live rooms with rapid-fire verification queues. The design language bridges high-engagement casino/arcade dynamism and sleek modern fintech utility.

### Visual Style
The aesthetic is a hybrid of **Neo-Arcade Modernism** and **Tactile Glassmorphic Depth**:
- Deep, immersive midnight-indigo environments for live game rooms keep visual strain low and turn high-contrast caller counters and ticket cells into radiant focal points.
- Clean, tactile card surfaces with refined borders, frosted glass modal overlays, and luminous jewel-toned accents deliver crisp readability.
- Kinetic excitement is maintained via vibrant glowing states, stamped dabber rings, and pulsating cues without descending into cheap, cluttered carnival tropes.
- The administrative side adapts the same palette into a high-density, authoritative dashboard prioritizing rapid verification workflows, numeric legibility, and operational control.

## Colors

The palette establishes two clear cognitive modes: high-immersion room tension and crisp transactional clarity.

- **Primary (`#4F46E5` - Electric Indigo):** Anchors game room headers, primary actionable states, room connection badges, and authenticated user navigation. Communicates structural authority, tech-forward gaming security, and game state anchors.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B` - Energetic Amber):** The universal jackpot and active-state currency. Reserved for high-thrill elements: dabbed cell rings, current active caller ball, prize banners (Early Five, Line Wins, Full House), countdown warning states, and coin balances.
- **Tertiary (`#10B981` - Emerald Green):** The token of success and verified claims. Dictates the 'Claim Prize' activation button, successfully stamped numbers, valid ticket claims, and positive financial balance changes.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A` - Midnight Slate):** The bedrock background for live caller views, ticket play spaces, and high-contrast ambient environments. 
- **Named Functional Colors:**
  - `crimson-danger` (`#EF4444`): Urgency countdowns (<30s), bogie/false claim penalization banners, and room disconnect alerts.
  - `indigo-surface` (`#1E1B4B`) & `surface-card` (`#1E293B`): Dual container tiers that layer tickets, admin tables, and ticket purchase drawers above the canvas.
  - `claimed-overlay` (`#312E81`): 60% translucent veil applied to tickets that have already exhausted claimable lines or been validated by hosts.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy utilizes three complementary typefaces:

1. **Outfit (Headlines & Dramatic Elements):** A geometric, modern sans-serif delivering punchy, friendly authority for section headers, winner announcements, jackpot totals, and modal titles.
2. **Plus Jakarta Sans (Body, Form Controls & System Text):** Clean, open letterforms engineered for quick scanning across dense user dashboard rows, prize criteria descriptors, legal disclaimers, and payment checkout prompts.
3. **Space Grotesk (Caller Ball Numbers, Ticket Grid Digits, Timers):** A technical, high-contrast monospace/geometric hybrid. With distinct zero slash, prominent numerals, and zero ambiguity between 6, 8, and 9, it prevents costly dabbing errors under high adrenaline.

Numeric figures in tickets, caller spheres, and countdown badges must always set `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` to guarantee grid alignment and jitter-free second ticking.

## Layout & Spacing

Layouts adhere to an adaptive fluid grid built around game-first ergonomic constraints.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column layout. Room gameplay stacks elements vertically: top fixed HUD with current caller cue and prize carousel, followed by a vertically scrollable deck of 3x9 player tickets, with an anchored bottom bar housing the primary 'Claim' trigger.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout. Split layout where the caller ball, prize matrix, and recent cue board take a 3-column sticky left rail, while player tickets display in a 5-column scrollable grid.
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column grid. Admin views utilize a full 12-column layout (4-column live controls, 8-column player validation and ticket monitors). Consumer room views adopt an ultra-wide split: Left side (4 cols) features caller history & prize claim logs; Center (5 cols) hosts active player tickets; Right side (3 cols) displays room chat, leaderboard, and audio/auto-dab settings.

### Spacing Rhythm
- Compact component padding (`space-xs` to `space-sm`) is strictly applied to 3x9 ticket grids to guarantee mobile visibility without horizontal scrolling.
- Generous outer margins (`margin-tablet`, `margin-desktop`) frame the dashboard and booking checkout surfaces, keeping critical actions centered within the thumb-reach zone on portable devices.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a combination of tonal layering, glowing jewel-edge highlights, and deep backdrop filters:

- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Flat `#0F172A` background simulating an arcade lounge environment.
- **Level 1 (Card & Board Containers):** Deep slate `#1E293B` bounded by a 1px soft border (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)`). Used for unselected tickets, prize summary cards, and admin data tables.
- **Level 2 (Active Interactive Elements & Sticky HUDs):** Indigo-tinted `#1E1B4B` featuring a diffused outer drop shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)`. Applies to caller spheres, ticket headers, and checkout modals.
- **Level 3 (High-Urgency Overlays & Won States):** Semi-transparent frosted layer (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.85)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(12px)`), bounded by an inner amber glow (`box-shadow: inset 0 0 16px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.25)`). Applied to 3-second claim confirmation banners, jackpot bursts, and OTP verification popups.
- **Dabber Rings & Number Balls:** Radial luminescence layered under numbers. Caller ball features an electric indigo-to-amber outer halo (`box-shadow: 0 0 32px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.45)`).

## Shapes

The system relies on structured **Level 2 (Rounded)** geometries to balance playfulness with orderly tabular data:

- **Standard Buttons & Inputs:** `rounded-lg` (0.75rem / 12px) creates smooth, touchable tap targets that feel native on iOS and Android viewports.
- **Ticket Cards:** Container boundary is `rounded-xl` (1rem / 16px), while internal 3x9 ticket cells use an inner `rounded-md` (0.375rem / 6px) to maintain a neat grid structure.
- **Number Caller Balls & Dabber Stamps:** Perfectly circular (`rounded-full`) to echo traditional wooden/acrylic bingo balls and felt ink stamps.
- **Prize Chips & System Status Badges:** Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`) with tight internal padding, framing numbers and status tags cleanly.

## Components

### 1. Tambola 3x9 Ticket Grid
- **Structure:** 3 rows by 9 columns. Exactly 5 numbers and 4 blank cells per row.
- **Empty Cells:** Muted background (`#0F172A` at 50% opacity) with a subtle diagonal pinstripe or micro-dot pattern.
- **Number Cells:** Elevated dark container (`#1E293B`) with high-contrast Space Grotesk white text (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Dabbed / Stamped State:** On call match or tap, a glowing amber circular stamp (`#F59E0B` with 20% alpha background and solid 2px outer border) overlays the number, accompanied by a brief pop animation (scale 1.15 to 1.0).
- **Claimed Ticket State:** When a winning claim is validated for this ticket, an elegant `#312E81` semi-transparent wash covers the card with a central gold checkmark badge displaying the won category (e.g., "TOP LINE WON").

### 2. Live Automated Caller Sphere
- **Display:** Central 72px (mobile: 56px) circular sphere with 3D gradient finish (`#4F46E5` transitioning to `#4338CA`), framed by a pulsating amber accent ring.
- **Motion:** When a new number drops, the sphere executes an entry zoom and pulse (`transform: scale(0.8) -> scale(1.05) -> scale(1)`), updating the Space Grotesk numerical readout.
- **Recent Cues Bar:** Horizontal row of the previous 5 called balls displayed in miniaturized pills (32px), with the oldest fading out to 40% opacity.

### 3. Prize Claim Matrix & Banners
- **Grid Categories:** Early Five, Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line, Four Corners, Full House.
- **Prize Cards:** Compact pills containing the prize title, payout cash amount or coin value, and live availability badge:
  - *Available:* Emerald outline with glowing status indicator.
  - *Claim In Review:* Amber pulse with "Host Verifying..." ticker.
  - *Claimed:* Muted grey slate with the winner's masked username/ticket ID.
- **Primary 'Claim Prize' Action Bar:** Fixed to the bottom viewport on mobile. Remains disabled until the system detects a valid winning combination on any player ticket, flashing an emerald glow when eligible to prevent accidental "bogie" claims.

### 4. Ticket Purchase & Booking Modal
- **Quantity Stepper:** Tactile counter with increment/decrement pills, bulk quick-select buttons (+1, +3, +6 / "Full Sheet").
- **Interactive Ticket Preview:** Horizontally swipeable deck of available ticket slips before confirmation.
- **OTP & Auth Flow:** Integrated split 6-digit pin input fields with automatic focus progression and countdown resend timer.
- **Payment Gateway Triggers:** Direct payment badges for UPI apps, QR scanner embed container, and card checkout buttons with SSL trust iconography.

### 5. Admin Live Control Console
- **Quick-Control Bar:** Large tactile buttons for "Draw Next Number", "Toggle Auto-Caller (Timer: 3s/5s/10s)", and emergency "Pause Game".
- **Dispute Resolution Queue:** Instant modal notification when a user hits 'Claim'. Displays the claimed ticket beside the live caller board, highlighting matching numbers with an instantaneous "Approve & Payout" or "Reject / Bogie Penalty" split button.
- **Ticket Generation Generator:** Numerical range inputs, bulk count selector, and download/print grid previews.
